Qualifying spots for the NCAA Championships

Each region gets 5 women's and 4 men's spots in every event, plus one for each diver from that region who finished in the top 16 of that event at the previous NCAA Championships. For the current zones this reproduces the NCAA's published 2026 and 2027 tables exactly.

How long each day would run

Events are paired the way Zones A, C and D ran them in 2026: women's 1m with men's 3m, women's 3m with men's 1m, then both platforms. Prelims are timed at 32 seconds a dive. Two panels means the two events run at the same time on separate boards; one panel means one after the other. Finals are 18 divers an event. Warm-ups are not included.

For scale, the 2026 prelims: Zone A women's 1m had 86 divers and men's 1m 55, and Zone A split its springboard prelims into two flights. The largest prelim run in one flight was 55 divers at Zone D.

How the zone fields scored against the standard

Counts the divers whose preliminary score at the zone meet reached a line you set, measured from the zone standard. Prelims use the same number of dives as the standard: women 265 (1m), 280 (3m), 225 (platform); men 300, 320, 300.

One-page summary of the map on screen

Print opens your print window. Choose Save as PDF there to make a PDF.

What is counted. A diver counts once per meet if they competed in at least one preliminary. Entries who scratched are not counted. Team events are not counted. The 2021 meets were held at combined sites with reduced fields. Schools are placed by the state of the institution; every school's state was checked against the zone meet it attended. Source: DiveMeets results for all 60 zone meets, 2015 to 2026.

Distances. Straight-line miles from each school's city to the host site. Road miles run longer. Averages are weighted by divers, so a school that brings eight divers counts eight times.
